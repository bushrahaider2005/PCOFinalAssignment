SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Fast learner

    I can quickly grasp concepts.

  • Dedicated

    I take my studies seriously and work hard to understand whats being taught.

  • Excellence

    I aim to gain the highest scores, not just the bare minimum.

Weaknesses

  • Interest

    I find subjects that I don't like more difficult, but thankfully my interests align well with my studies.

  • Exhaustion

    The dedication on my studies can sometimes take away from my social life. During exam periods I would have little to no social life

Opportunities

  • Internships

    Performing an internship is a great opportunity to gain practical and industry experience, which will be useful for my future jobs.

  • Minor Study

    The minor is also a strong point for me as it allows me to study a slightly different but still related study for a semester, allowing me to expand my knowledge and skill sets.

Threats

  • Burn out

    Sometimes I am worried about working too much that it could impact my mental health and I wouldn't be able to perform as well in my studies.

  • My headaches

    I have an undiagnosed headache that randomly appears from time to time, and sometimes it comes at the worst time when I need to focus and study for example during exam periods. And it decreases my ability to study and perform well.

SMART Goals

  1. Along side my classes I will put aside 18 hours per week to revise what I have learned and practice it. So that by the end of the year I have guaranteed a 100% pass rate in all my classes.
  2. I will make sure to keep a balance between study and my personal or social life, I will make sure to not go over my 18 hours of extra study, I will always keep my saturdays free to have some fun and relax and I will dedicate 2 hours daily for mind refreshing activities, such as walking, going to the park, reading and gaming. Following this will make sure that by the end of the year I will not encounter any burn outs or loss of interest for study.

Mission

My main mission for this year is 100% completion of all my subjects as well as achieving the highest grades possible, all whilst keeping a good balance between my study and free time.
