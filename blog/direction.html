Directions

I am still in my first term of ICT, so it is still quite early to decide what I will be doing in 4 years but I believe I may have figured out the direction of ICT study I may want to follow. My initial interest was in software development and continues to be that, I will try to expand my skills as much as possible in that field. I also believe that combining it with another direction like Cloud and Infrastructure could be very powerful, not only will I have the skill sets to create the software solutions but also the ability to deploy it in a good manner. I have chosen these directions because of it's alignment with my interests and motivations.

Me in 4 years!

After 4 years at ICT I expect to be a full stack software developer with knowledge and experience in working with different languages, frameworks, libraries and tools. I expect to have had professional industry experience with the help of the internships in year 3. I believe that after graduating I should have built a good enough of a portfolio to be able to start applying for real jobs in the market. I might be able to start off as a junior software developer for a small start up somewhere and build my skills and career there.
