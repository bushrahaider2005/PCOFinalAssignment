My Programming Experience

My experience with programming at ICT has been quite straight forward, even though I arrived about 3 weeks late to the study, I still managed to catch up and follow the lessons along side the other students. Many programming concepts were completely new to me and understanding them wasn't really the hard part, but knowing when to use what, that became a bit of a challenge for me. But I know that with a little more practice, I will get the hang of it's usage too.
One specific concept that I am still struggling with fully understanding it and being able to use it effectively is recursion. It is hard to understand the flow of variables and it does not really work like a while loop, but it is also quite similar.
Recently I took the mock exam for my programming class, it did not go very well, at the time of the exam I thought to myself that the exam is extremely hard, and I had no idea what I was supposed to be doing. But looking back at it, the exam itself as in the programming problems were not that difficult, but being able to understand the problem description and understanding what is being asked to do, that was the hard part. Looking back at the exam, there weren't anything new that was not covered during classes, they were all the same concepts but just the issue is that we have to figure out which programming concepts we have to use to solve the problem. So to prepare for the real exam, I have looked through and reviewed all our class material and tried to identify which kind of problems there are and what programming concept should be used for each.
