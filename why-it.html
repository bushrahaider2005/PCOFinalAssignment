Golden Circle

Why?

I am studying ICT because it is my wish to become a software developer, ever since graduating from highschool I have been trying to direct myself towards this career path. And today I am on it!

How?

I will achieve my goals by following the ICT program and dedicating time for self study so that I am able to learn as much as possible for the time being. What sets me apart is learning new tools and languages along side the ICT study.

What?

I have decided to follow my initial wishes of becoming a software developer, so I expect to continue in this field and excel at it.

My Motivation

At the time of my graduation from highschool in 2021 I was already interested in programming and ICT like fields but I could not immediately start with university due to unfortunate timing. In 2021, the government of my home country (Afghanistan) was overruled and the new government's first step was to attack women's education. They started with banning women and girls from University and then also from highschool. I was devastated and my dreams of receiving my education shattered before my eyes. Regardless I was thankful that I had my highschool diploma and I started to do some self study. My programming journey started with SheCodes (an online learning platform for women wanting to get into programming) it was great and I learned most of my HTML and CSS skills from there. Details aside eventually I managed to make it here and pursue my dreams. Looking back at what it would have been had I stayed there and what I had to go through to be able to be here today gives me all the motivation I need. I am beyond grateful to have the ability to receive this high quality education in the field that I wanted to study.

Information and Communication Technology - ICT

What do I know about the field?

The ICT field is for those who want to be an all round software engineer or data scientist. In the first year we get a taste of both sides, which would allow us to make a choice for our second year which role we want to pursue. In our first year we have classes like Programming Basics, Object oriented programming, Software Engineering Basics and Web Application Development to give us the foundational knowledge to become a Software Engineer. And on the other hand some of these classes would also be needed for Data Science but it also has a few essential classes to get us started as a Data Scientist.

What are my expectations?

After studying ICT at HZ for one term, it allowed me to set my expectations for the future and what I can expect in the coming semesters and years.
I believe that we are going at pace that is just right for our ability of understanding and performing, had it been faster or harder, we would have fallen behind, but at this pace we are challenged and we have to stay focused and study to keep up. I expect to receive the same level of high quality education for the coming semesters, I believe at this pace we will be able to learn a lot and be at a point where we can create programs, application or websites by the end of the year.

What do I want to achieve?

Mainly I want to make sure that I can pass all my classes with exceptional grades, that would be my main goal. But along side I would like to expand my skills as a software developer, once I get to a point where I can comfortably do programming, then at that point I would like to try to learn new languages or tools that are not taught by the ICT program. I believe having a vast skill set will allow me to be able to find an internship easier in my 3rd year. And it can also support my future professional career after graduation.

What do I want to learn?

As perviously mentioned, I wish to learn all that the ICT program at HZ has to offer and in my free time I would like to learn new languages and tools that are commonly used in today's job market so that I can be ready for my internships and future jobs. I also believe that the internships themselves will be a great place to grow my professional career as I will get industry experience and also maybe learn something new and useful.
